Slide 05 • Editorial Strategy

Content Strategy: Emotional Conflict Wins; Niche Formats Disconnect

High-stakes social controversy drove 500K+ views and 7% CTRs, while spiritual podcasts struggled below 3.5% CTR and drama recaps faced Content ID blocks.

✓ What Worked: The Flagship Formula6.6%–7.3% CTR
Nintha Khao dominated via post-broadcast companionship and high-stakes human drama.
  • Top 5 Videos Alone >2.6M Views: Immediate debriefs following live national TV broadcasts (Hone Krasae) captured pre-heated emotional investment.
  • Optimal Runtime (20–40 min): Extended conversational pacing delivered 8–15 min AVD and rich mid-roll ad inventory.
  • Personal Betrayal & Confrontation: Leaked audio, family feuds, and public clashes consistently pushed CTR above 6.5%–7.3%.
⚠ What Underperformed: Root FrictionNeeds Refactoring
Intent mismatch, copyright leakage, and passive framing capped non-core formats.
  • SEERCRET PODCAST (3 eps | 10.3K views | $2.81): Astrology/horoscope interviews clashed with news-seeking subscribers, dropping CTR to 3.12%.
  • Nintha Series (13 eps | 209.2K views | $46.21): Solid 5.59% CTR, but 6 of 13 episodes were demonetized ($0.00) by Content ID claims on drama footage.
  • Passive/Technical News: Regulatory disputes (e.g., RAAT Go-Kart drama) dropped CTR to 1.2%–2.0%, cutting views by over 50%.
2027 Editorial Greenlight Rules: (1) Require personal tension or direct conflict to clear the 5.7% CTR benchmark; (2) Reframe spiritual/lifestyle shows around real-world controversies or fraud exposés; (3) Preserve 25–35 min flagship runtimes to protect 10+ min AVD.
